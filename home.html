The Wright Estate
The Wright Estate is a condominium community established in 1988 on the grounds of the Wright Estate Mansion on Court Street in Keene.
We feature 89-units surrounded by woods and scenic views, just one mile north of downtown Keene. Amenities include a small clubhouse, trash compactor and a tennis court. Our grounds are meticulously maintained and the entire complex is quiet and serene.
